Cancellation & Satisfaction Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

We want every visit to be easy to plan and every result to be spotless. Here’s how cancellations, deposits and our satisfaction guarantee work.

24 hoursFree cancellation or rescheduling with at least 24 hours’ notice
$40Fee for cancellations under 24 hours or if we can’t get in
Free re-cleanTell us within 24 hours if anything was missed

Cancelling or rescheduling

  • 24 hours or more before your appointment: cancel or reschedule for free. Your deposit is refunded, or carried over to your new date if you reschedule.
  • Less than 24 hours before your appointment: a $40 late-cancellation fee applies. We deduct it from your deposit and refund the rest.

Lockouts

If our team arrives at the scheduled time and cannot get in (no one home, wrong access code, keys not available) or cannot safely work, it counts as a late cancellation and the $40 fee applies.

If we need to cancel

If we have to cancel or reschedule (for example because of severe weather, illness or an emergency), we will contact you as soon as possible and offer the next available time or a full refund of your deposit. No fee applies to you.

Our satisfaction guarantee (free re-clean)

  • If you’re not happy with any part of the cleaning, let us know within 24 hours of the visit, ideally with photos of the areas in question.
  • We will come back and re-clean those areas for free, at a time that works for both of us.
  • The guarantee covers the services included in your booking. It doesn’t cover areas that were not part of the agreed scope, or areas that were used or became dirty again after our visit.
  • Because we fix issues with a free re-clean, we don’t give cash refunds for completed cleanings.

Refunds of deposits

Deposit refunds are sent back using the same payment method, usually within 5 business days.
