Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

K.R.&G.M. Cleaning LLC (“we”, “us”, “our”) runs this website, www.krandgmcleaning.com. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact and quote requests: your name, email address, phone number, your message, and the options you choose in our estimate calculator (type of cleaning, number of rooms, home size, extras, any discount code and the estimated price).
  • Newsletter sign-up: your email address.
  • When you book: your service address, access instructions and payment details you share with us directly.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: when you send a form we store your browser type (user agent). Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability.
  • Browser storage: we save two small preferences in your browser’s local storage — whether you already subscribed, and when you closed our welcome offer — so we don’t show you the pop-up again. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking or analytics.

How we use your information

  • To answer your questions, prepare quotes, schedule and provide cleaning services, and take payment.
  • To send you our newsletter and offers if you subscribed. Every marketing email lets you unsubscribe, or you can simply ask us.
  • To keep our website secure and prevent spam and abuse.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Who we share it with

We use a few trusted service providers who process data for us only to run this website:

  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • Supabase — secure database where form submissions are stored.
  • Resend — delivers the email notifications about your request to our inbox.
  • Google Fonts — provides the fonts on this site; your browser connects to Google to load them.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, our staff or our customers.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and customer records for as long as needed to provide our services and meet tax and accounting requirements, and newsletter emails until you unsubscribe. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

Security

Our site uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), and our database only accepts new submissions from the website — it cannot be read publicly. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have given us, or to stop sending you marketing emails. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed.
