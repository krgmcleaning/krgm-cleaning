Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

K.R.&G.M. Cleaning LLC (“we”, “us”) operates this website. This page explains what information we collect through it and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you use our contact form or the cleaning estimate calculator, we collect the details you choose to send us: your name, email address, phone number, your message, and the options you selected in the calculator. If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your request, prepare a quote, schedule and provide cleaning services, and — if you subscribed — to send you our offers. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any of our emails.

How it is stored

Form submissions are stored securely with our hosting and database providers (Vercel and Supabase) and may be delivered to our inbox through an email service provider. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you sent us by contacting us through the contact form.
